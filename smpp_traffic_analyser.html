SMPP Traffic Analyser

This on-line "SMPP" traffic analyser parses a "pcap" / "pcapng" packet capture (e.g. from "tcpdump" or "Wireshark") containing "SMPP" over "TCP", reassembles every connection (channel) and matches each request with its response by sequence number, so requests sent without waiting for an acknowledgement (an "SMPP" window) and out-of-order responses are handled. Per channel and for all channels combined, it reports the request / response latency (distribution and over time), the SMS rate (messages submitted per second), the numbers of messages submitted (MT-SMS), acknowledged and received (MO-SMS), delivery receipts, errors and unanswered requests, statistics per "PDU" type, the fastest and slowest responses, the peak rate and the outstanding requests (the "SMPP" window actually used). The capture is analysed locally in your browser and is never uploaded.
 
References

  • SMPP Protocol Specification v3.4
Privacy: the capture file is analysed locally in your browser. No data is uploaded or sent to any remote site.
