sms / smpp tools

web apps written in "JavaScript" for troubleshooting in the area of "SMS / SMPP" messages. They run entirely in your browser: nothing you enter or open is uploaded.

  • SMS Message Analyser - encodes a message string according to the "GSM default" or "UCS2" alphabet and displays the hex data-stream representation of the encoded message. In case of a "long" SMS, it also splits the "encoded" message and presents the encoded "user data" message parts along with the proper "UDH" fields. A utility developed in 2014, while working for "Persado" as a "Connectivity" engineer.
  • SMPP Traffic Analyser - loads a "pcap" / "pcapng" capture of "SMPP" over "TCP", reassembles every connection (channel) and matches each request with its response, in order to chart the request / response latency and the SMS rate and to report statistics on submitted (MT-SMS, "submit_sm"), received (MO-SMS, "deliver_sm") and delivery receipt messages, error codes, the fastest and slowest PDUs and the "SMPP" window used, per channel and for all channels combined.

mathematical & statistical tools

interactive tools that run in your browser, originally developed as "Java" applets as part of the MSc. in "Telecommunication and Information Systems" course work during the 1997 - 98 academic year, which was offered by the Electronic Systems Engineering department of the University of Essex. They are now plain web pages written in "JavaScript", so no "Java" plug-in is needed any more.

  • Erlang's Loss Formula - the theory behind the "Erlang B" formula and an interactive calculator of loss (blocking) probabilities.
  • Statistical Analysis of Experimental Data - opens a file of X,Y observation pairs and presents the descriptive statistics, a scatter plot with a least-squares regression model (up to 5th degree), regression diagnostics and data validation. The file is processed locally in your browser.
